Section 03
Why a Single-Page, Single-Project Site

The Core Problem With the Old Site

kb.mahalaxmiinfra.in spreads attention across 15 projects, a blog, testimonials, and a generic FAQ. For paid traffic aimed at one project, this dilutes intent and lowers conversion. This plan keeps every element the old site does well (trust stats, amenities grid, testimonials, FAQ, contact form) but re-points all of it at Nagar-45 only.

Kept From Old Site

Sticky enquire button, amenities grid with icons, testimonials with photos, FAQ accordion, click-to-call and WhatsApp links, Google Maps embed, footer with RERA number.

Removed / Deferred

Multi-project portfolio grid (moved to a collapsed footer section), blog/news section, generic "Why Choose Us" icon grid replaced with Nagar-45-specific investment story, generic stock-style amenity icons replaced with real render photography.

Guiding Principle

Every section on the page should answer one of three questions for the visitor: What is this project? Why is this location a good investment? Why should I enquire right now?

Flow Rationale

This order matches the recommended paid-traffic flow: Hero → Trust → Location → Highlights → Investment Potential → Real Photos → Testimonials → FAQ → Lead Form → Final CTA. The enquiry form is pulled up right after FAQ instead of buried at the very bottom, and sticky buttons mean a visitor never has to scroll to find a way to enquire.

Website Structure: Navigation Tabs

Why a Condensed Tab Bar

The page has 14 stacked sections (see the flow above), but a full-menu navbar listing all of them overwhelms a visitor and dilutes the one goal — enquiry. The sticky nav instead surfaces only six jump-to tabs for the sections a buyer actually searches for, and quietly folds the rest (Marquee, Trust Bar, Investment Story, Scarcity, Site Photos, Testimonials, Final CTA) into the natural scroll — reachable, just not competing for nav space.

WhatsApp Funnel

Persistent floating WhatsApp icon (bottom-right on desktop). Pre-filled message: "Get Price List on WhatsApp." Real estate enquiries convert heavily on WhatsApp — keep this visible in every section.

Lead Magnets

Section 08
SEO & Technical Notes
Page Title & Meta

Title: "Mahalaxmi Nagar-45 | NMRDA Approved Plots Near Samruddhi Circle, Nagpur." Meta description should mention AIIMS, IIM, MIHAN proximity and bank finance, matching the hero copy.

Performance

Compress all renders/photos to WebP, under 200KB each. Lazy-load below-the-fold images. Target under 3 seconds load on 4G, since this page is built for paid traffic.

Tracking

Install GA4 and Meta/Google Ads pixels on Day 1. Track conversion events separately: price-list form submits, WhatsApp clicks, call clicks, and layout-plan downloads.

Legal & Compliance

Display both MahaRERA numbers (PP1190002501447, PP1190002501287) in the footer with QR codes linking to maharera.maharashtra.gov.in, matching the brochure back cover.

3. Sticky Lead Form — Behaviour Spec
📌 This is distinct from the sticky nav buttons in Section 06 — it is an actual inline form (not just a button) that appears the moment the Hero loads, so a ready-to-buy visitor never has to hunt for the enquiry form even on their first screen.
Desktop (>1024px)
  • Compact 3-field card (Name, Phone, "Get Price List" button) docked to the right edge of the Hero
  • On scroll past Hero, it un-docks and sticks to the bottom-right of the viewport as a slim floating card
  • Collapses to a single gold pill "Get Price List" once the visitor reaches the full Lead Form section (#enquire), so it never duplicates the form on-screen
Mobile (<768px)
  • Inline compact form sits directly under the Hero buttons — no separate float needed above the fold
  • From section 2 onward, it hands off to the fixed Mobile Sticky Bar (Call · Price List · WhatsApp) defined in Section 06
  • Tapping "Price List" on the sticky bar opens the same form as a bottom sheet, pre-filled with nothing, 2 fields only (Name, Phone) to reduce friction
