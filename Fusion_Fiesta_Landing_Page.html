Competition Rules

THE RULES OF THE KITCHEN

Simple, fair and designed to let your cooking do the talking.

01
Open to All Skill Levels
Home cooks, culinary students and amateur chefs all welcome. No professional credentials required.
02
Time Limit: 45 Minutes
Each participant gets 45 minutes to prepare and plate their dish. Speed Round is 30 minutes.

Ingredients Provided

Judging Criteria
Taste (50%), Presentation (30%), Originality (20%). Judges' decision is final.

One Category Per Participant
Each registration covers one category. Multiple registrations allowed for different categories.
06
Hygiene is Mandatory
Aprons and hair nets provided. Participants must follow kitchen hygiene protocols at all times.
