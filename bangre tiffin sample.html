Authentic Homestyle Pedagogy

Prepared by Sudha Bangre (Aunty Ji)

Bangre Tiffin was born out of Sudha Aunty's kitchen in 2019. Frustrated by the oily, unhygienic food served in commercial messes across Nagpur, she started cooking homestyle meals for students and young working professionals.

Today, we cook for over 1,800+ happy subscribers daily. We use 100% pure cow desi ghee, hand-ground spices, zero artificial food colors, and no MSG. Every tiffin is packed in hot insulated stainless steel containers.
