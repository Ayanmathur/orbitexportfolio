FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply?

12th pass in Science, Commerce, or Arts (or equivalent Diploma/ITI) with minimum 50% aggregate. Preference given to candidates with national-level certifications in drawing, dance, photography, sculpting, or modeling.

What are the career options after this course?

Self-employment/own label, or roles with export houses, garment store chains, textile mills, leather companies, boutiques, fashion show organizers, jewelry houses, and media houses.

Are scholarships available?

Yes, scholarships are available for eligible candidates — details confirmed during counselling.

What facilities does the college offer for this course?

Modern sketch lab, industry-standard multimedia lab, and a dedicated photography/shooting floor for practical training.

What other courses does Umang Geetai College offer?

Alongside this program, the college offers B.Sc. Home Science with specializations in Fashion & Apparel Design and Nutrition & Dietetics, plus Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.) — all under S.N.D.T. Women's University, Mumbai.

What is the admission process?

Fill out the enquiry form on this page or call/WhatsApp the admissions team. A counsellor will guide you through eligibility, documents, and enrolment steps for your chosen course.

Are scholarships available across all courses?

Yes, scholarships are available for eligible candidates across all programs — Fashion & Apparel Design, Nutrition & Dietetics, and B.Com. Details are confirmed during counselling.
